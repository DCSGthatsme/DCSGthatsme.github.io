Amber Cress is the 22-year-old younger sister of [[Scarlet]], originally hailing from the small village of [[Diago]]. She has strawberry blonde hair, typically tied in a bun with shears from her time working on her sister's watermelon farm. Although she supported Scarlet's farming endeavor near [[Brio]], she became emotionally distant before mysteriously disappearing during the farm's second year. Unknown to Scarlet, Amber had been seeking solitude to secretly experiment with [[Sophistry]]. Following a chance meeting with [[Agnivisti]], a molten [[Principles|Principle]], she decided to accompany it. She eventually found her way to [[Ferrun]], where she worked as a miner. When the party found her in the mines there, they confronted the Principle in combat and lost. Agnivisti left Ferrun after razing it to the ground, and Amber chose again to follow.
