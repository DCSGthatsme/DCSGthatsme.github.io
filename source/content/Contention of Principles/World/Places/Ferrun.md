Ferrun was a village renowned worldwide for its metalwork and stonework. Chieftain of Ferrun was Master Craftsman Bolvar.

As part of a deal, Ferrun began to focus on metal exportation to [[Ekklesbraght]]. To assist in their demands, Ekkelsbraght granted Ferrun advanced alchemical blast stones. Operations were eventually halted when the mines began to bleed lava when struck.

The [[Party|party]] arrived in Ferrun to search for [[Amber]]. When they found Amber deep in the mines, they also found [[Agnivisti]]. After a battle with the [[Principles|Principle]] led to the party's defeat, Agnivisti and its minions destroyed the village. Its tribe began a mass evacuation to [[Tribute]].
