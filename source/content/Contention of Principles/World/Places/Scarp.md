Scarp is a small hamlet characterized by its precarious yet strategic position atop a defensive escarpment.

Scarp was overrun by a massive, flying, bat-like [[Principles|Principle]] and its minions whose ability to bypass the village's natural cliff-side defenses caught the residents unawares. The village's thatched-roof houses had been largely destroyed, caving inward in a ring-shaped pattern concentrated toward the center of the town.

As a consequence, Scarp is currently a ghost town; its tribe was forced to flee to the nearby town of [[Heed]]. Tragically, when Heed was destroyed, few of those tribespersons survived.
