Ekklesbraght, the world's first and only city, is a center of unprecedented expansion and advancement.

Ekklesbraght's growth has created a high demand for metals, driving the intensive mining operations found in [[Ferrun]].

The [[Reason]] originates in Ekklesbraght as a product of their local culture. Ekklesbraght is the source of the the [[Eikavox|eikavox]] gifted to [[Heed]] that is now in the party's possession. The blast stones used by the miners of Ferrun also originated from the city.
