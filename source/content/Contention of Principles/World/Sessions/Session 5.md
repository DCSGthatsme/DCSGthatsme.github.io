The hand wreathed in lava reaches out through the stone wall, fully emerging as a humanoid entity. Either made of or producing cascading lava, it has the same rough size and shape of a man. The creature is non-hostile and appears to be performing a task, carrying a large, cuboid, crystalline metal chunk. Misha, the mine supervisor, flees in terror while [[Kaelen]] attempts communication. The creature ignores initial queries but shakes its head "no" when asked for help or if the party can follow it, though it nods "yes" when asked if it has a name. [[Bodin]] throws a rock at the creature, which embeds in its sticky, molten surface without provoking a reaction. The figure eventually carves a molten doorway into a wall and vanishes into a cavern containing a lava river.

Realizing they are ill-equipped for the extreme heat, the party retreats to the surface to report to Chieftain Bolvar. They describe the creature and the self-mending walls of the mine. The party spends the rest of the day preparing for a deeper descent. Bodin and [[Marko]] barter for two sets of heat-resistant robes at a house marked with a spider sigil, trading a day's labor and a mining helmet. Marko visits a metalworker and trades a steel shield for a [[Parrying Dagger|parrying dagger]] and some [[Yanzifei|yanzifei]]. [[Owen]] attempts to create herbal cures and antidotes through [[Alchemy]]. His use of [[Sophistry]] results in temporary [[Corruption]], causing a recurring hand rash and a period of hydrophobia wherein the sight of water is repulsive to him. Kaelen and [[Heka]] interview a young man named Galen, who identifies three missing friends - Jadwa, Haytham, and Tam - who disappeared after discovering an unexplored, resource-rich cavern.

The following morning, the party re-enters the mines equipped with their new gear. They are intercepted by a small bat that attempts to bite Scarlet before leading them through the tunnels. Following the bat's guidance, Bodin uses a pickaxe to breach several mending walls, eventually breaking into a massive, spherical cavern filled with a "garden" of diverse crystals and minerals.

Inside the chamber, they find [[Scarlet|Scarlet's]] missing sister, [[Amber]], conscious but slumped against a wall. Lined up next to her are the three missing youths who are in a non-responsive, coma-like state but remain alive. Amber tearfully reunites with Scarlet. However, Amber refuses to leave, claiming she has to "help him" and that she has talked "him" down from destroying the town. Suddenly, a massive magma creature, twenty times larger than the first, erupts from the lava river.
