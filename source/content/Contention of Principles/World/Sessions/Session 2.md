Outside of the ruins of Tanji's cellar in the hamlet of [[Scarp]], the dust of the collapse settles. The party has caught the attention of the [[Principles|Principle's]] minions, and two large, bat-like creatures with hornet-like stingers descend from the sky to attack the group.

The initial skirmish is frantic; [[Marko]] is immediately stung and poisoned, while [[Bodin]], [[Scarlet]], and [[Heka]] engage the creatures in a desperate melee. [[Owen]] provides ranged support, successfully felling one of the monsters with a well-placed [[Arrow|arrow]]. However, just as the party begins to thin the ranks of the smaller minions, a house-sized, four-winged bat creature - the Principle itself - descends and casts a massive shadow over the group. The creature knocks Scarlet prone and poisons [[Kaelen]] before Bodin delivers a massive, berserker-fueled blow with his [[Halberd|halberd]], severing one of the Principle's four wings. Badly wounded and intimidated by the party's ferocity, the giant creature flees toward the nearby cliff face.

After the battle, Heka uses his medical skills to tend to the group's wounds while they observe the giant creature fly over the highest cliff tier and plummet into a hidden depression. Before pursuing it, the party investigates the dead minions, discovering that while their bodies are heavily armored with bristly fur, their wings are a significant vulnerability. Following a brief disagreement between Bodin and Scarlet regarding a severed talon taken as a trophy, the group cautiously searches the rest of Scarp. They find the hamlet abandoned, with many houses crushed inward in a strange, ring-shaped pattern concentrated toward the center of town.

The party eventually locates climbing equipment and scales the cliff to reach the depression where the giant bat was seen falling. At the top, they find a massive burrowed hole in the rock. Lighting torches, they descend into a lightless tunnel and are intercepted by three more house-sized bats. During this fight, Katla and Kaelen realize these massive opponents are merely [[Mirroring|mirror images]] created by the Principle to distract them.

Pushing deeper into the tunnels, the group finds a bizarre trail of circular objects - wagon wheels, millstones, and barrel hoops - strewn about the rock floor. This trail leads to a massive chamber containing a hoard of yet more circular junk. There, they confront the real Principle and its remaining minions in a final stand. The battle is a brutal struggle as the Principle spawns more illusions and its minions poison and paralyze several party members. Eventually, Marko manages to slide beneath the creature's wings and deliver a killing blow to its neck.

As the Principle dies, its remaining minions wither away into dust. In the moment of its demise, the entire party receives a series of psychic flashes: images of sunsets, wagons in transit, and people traveling across the countryside. After a final search of the circular horde, where Heka discovers a [[Steel Shield|steel shield]], the party spends a night resting in a relatively intact house in Scarp. The next morning, finding the hamlet safe and still, they begin the long trek back to Heed to report their success. However, as they approach the village, their relief turns to dread as they see thick plumes of smoke rising from the direction of Heed.
