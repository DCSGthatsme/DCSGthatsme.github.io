Nine days after the destruction of [[Ferrun]], at a refugee camp in the nearby foothills, the party fully awakens. They discover they share hazy, dream-like memories of performing forced manual labor - moving stones and having their energy drawn - during their period of unconsciousness.

[[Scarlet]] receives a significant revelation when [[Kaelen]] hands her the copper horse figurine dropped by [[Amber|Amber's]] bat [[Familiar|familiar]]. Upon touching it, she experiences a [[Flawless Record|message]] from Amber in her mind. Scarlet sees Amber in the reflection of a pool from a lush clearing as if seeing through Amber's eyes. Amber apologizes for running away, revealing she had been regularly seeking solitude in order to study [[Sophistry]] in private. However, after a chance encounter with a molten Principle who she calls "Agnivisti", she decided to leave for a time to accompany the Principle and see that story to its end. She promises to return to their home village, [[Diago]], for the Dressage Festival later that year.

Led by Tusk Damien and a group of [[Ardent Hearts|Ardent Heart]] knights, the roughly 200 survivors begin a slow migration toward [[Tribute]], a trade nexus deeper in the mountains. To assist the community, [[Bodin]], [[Owen]], and Scarlet hunt six wild boars, while [[Heka]] provides medical aid. Kaelen attempts to shirk responsibility through delegation but is eventually assigned to menial tasks. Marko speaks with a caravan worker and learns of a novel concept - within [[Ekklesbraght]], they exchange intricately patterned platinum trinkets called "Reasons". Since the [[Ekklesbraght Phrontistery]] guarantees that each Reason can be exchanged for [[Days' Rations|rations]], they are recognized as having abstract value and are traded as such within Ekklesbraght. Inscribed on every Reason are the phrases "light from reason" and "reason from light". Bodin attempts to smooth over relations with Chieftain Bolvar, who remains resigned but appreciative of the party's effort to help.

The party experiences a surreal, shared dream where they are transformed into five-year-old children (except for Heka, who remains as he is) living in a cozy mountain cottage. A giant hen, known as "Mother," feeds them a torrent of worms and tasks them with finding their "brother," Truffle. They trek through deep snow to find Truffle - a giant chick with a broken leg based on an animal companion Owen briefly had in his real-life childhood. After Heka splints the leg and they return to the cottage, the dream ends.

The following night, a second shared dream casts the party as youths in Owen's home village of [[Gonaparted]]. They find themselves bound before a council of elders, facing Owen's real-life past: he is on trial for practicing Sophistry ([[Alchemy]]) and endangering the tribe after a local boy, Nathan, was burned by his creations. As punishment, the council orders Owen to apologize face-to-face to every member of the tribe. The party abides the social gauntlet of shame before waking up in the cold mountain air.

On their final night before reaching Tribute, the party enters a third, more lucid dream. They find themselves in a pitch-black, stone chamber filled with a rhythmic thrumming sound. Working together, they push open a massive door to reveal a central chamber bathed in a faint red light. In the center, a giant, pulsating human heart is suspended in the air, with a colossal black serpent coiled around it.
