In a crystal cavern in the mines of [[Ferrun]], a massive magma [[Principles|Principle]] erupts from the lava river. As the party stands stunned, smaller lava minions begin emerging from the cavern walls to surround them.

The cavern contains four large crystals that pulse with color, shifting from blue to a vibrant red as the creature's anger grows. [[Bodin]], suspecting the crystals are the source of the creature's power or a physical weakness, begins attacking them with a pickaxe. Each shattered crystal causes a short burst of eruption from the Principle. Despite [[Amber|Amber's]] many warnings, Bodin continues his destruction until the Principle snaps; the conflict has escalated into a full-fledged fight.

[[Scarlet]] is the first to fall, knocked unconscious by the creatures' [[Effluence|effluence]] of heat and physical strikes. [[Heka]] attempts to rescue her but is [[Grappling Tongue|grappled by a molten tendril]] and pulled toward the Principle, falling unconscious and experiencing a sudden, intense flash of memory regarding his past in [[Aerasi]]. The conscious few of the party realize a horrifying pattern - whenever an ally falls, a new lava minion emerges from the river to join the fight. Bodin, [[Owen]], and [[Katla]] are eventually overwhelmed by the combined heat and the Principle's [[Deadly Breath|breath]].

[[Kaelen]] and [[Marko]] focus on extracting the missing miners. Amid the chaos, Kaelen successfully shoves the three unconscious youths through a closing portal in the wall. Kaelen and Marko manage to leap through the portal just as it seals, leaving their companions and Amber trapped inside with the Principle.

Kaelen and Marko return to the surface only to find Ferrun in a state of catastrophe. The mines are collapsing and the town is being overrun by lava creatures. Chieftain Bolvar initiates a mass evacuation toward the mountain settlement of [[Tribute]]. Marko and Kaelen lead a desperate fighting retreat at the town gates, holding the line long enough for the survivors to escape, though several fighters of Ferrun are lost in the fray.

Nine days of recovery follow at a camp in the foothills. A group of [[Ardent Hearts|Ardent Heart]] knights, led by Tusk Damien, arrives to assist the refugees. Kaelen and Marko return to the ruins of Ferrun with accompaniment from a few Claws of the Ardent Hearts to search the mines. They find Bodin, Heka, Owen, Scarlet, and Katla unconscious but alive near the former entrance to the crystal chamber. Amber, however, is missing once again. A small bat drops a copper horse figurine into Kaelen's hands. Heka also uses a [[Ritual Codex|ritual codex]] gifted to him by Amber to perform a [[Rituals|Sophistic ritual]], seeing a [[Clairvoyance|vision]] of the cavern where the party did battle with the Principle. He discovers the area is completely restored, appearing peaceful and untouched, though the creatures and Amber are gone.
