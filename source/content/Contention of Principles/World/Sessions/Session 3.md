The party arrives in [[Heed]], only to find the village has been completely destroyed. The ruins appear as if the earth itself was tilled, mixing debris, dirt, and fire in a localized but intentional disaster. Searching the rubble, the group discovers many dead refugees and eventually identifies the bodies of Chieftain Sthuna and Yaku, the chronicler.

While investigating the longhouse, which is partially collapsed and ablaze, [[Kaelen]] and [[Heka]] decide to retrieve the [[eikavox]] from the chieftain's office. [[Bodin]] uses his strength to lift a heavy log, allowing them entry. Inside the office, they find the room in disarray as if by an earthquake, with papers strewn everywhere. They recover the eikavox, which is still faintly playing harpsichord music until they close its doors. Kaelen also retrieves silver bands from the bodies of the leaders before Heka provides them with a respectful burial.

During the search, Marko uses his tracking skills to identify heavy, metal-shoed boot prints leading west toward the river. However, before following the tracks, the group is confronted by a massive, isopod-like creature with terracotta plates and long, curled antennae; a [[Principles|Principle]]. It remains perfectly still but communicates through a series of psychic visions shared with the party. These flashes include a black mist crashing through a forest, a stampede of cattle running into a rising sun, and a vision of miners working near lava. [[Scarlet]] recognizes one of the miners as her missing sister, [[Amber]]. The isopodan Principle extends an extrasensory invitation to each member of the party, allowing them the choice to form a [[Principle Bonds|Principle Bond]]. Then, it spurs into sudden action, leaping a massive distance toward the north, out of sight.

The party eventually follows the heavy tracks to the banks of the river, where they encounter a group of [[Ardent Hearts|Ardent Heart]] knights assisting ten survivors from [[Scarp]] and Heed. Though the lead knight, ranked Horn, is initially hostile and secretive, Kaelen uses his [[Charisma|charisma]] and knowledge of their titles to ease tensions. The knights reveal that on an unrelated mission four days prior, they came to learn that Heed's militia had perished one and all. A survivor from Heed shares that just before the earth began to boil and destroy the village, Yaku was seen attempting to start a fire on the east side, likely trying to invoke the protection mentioned in his legend.
