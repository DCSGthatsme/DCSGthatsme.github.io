The story begins in the walled village of [[Heed]] in autumn. [[Marko]] is the first to arrive, heading for the village longhouse, followed by [[Bodin]] and his protégé [[Katla]], [[Kaelen]], [[Scarlet]], [[Owen]], and finally [[Heka]], who had already been in town for several days. Inside the longhouse, they are met by Yaku, Heed's vivacious chronicler, who shares the importance of the creek running beneath the building and explains that the chieftain is currently busy.

To pass the time, Yaku tells a local legend. Central to the story are the legendary heroes - Godri the Warrior, Helen the Seer, Dexion the Chronicler, Arric the Pathfinder, Wym the Astronomer, and Cerise the Sentinel. These figures defend themselves atop a giant mesa against a swarm of winged serpents and a massive, subterranean burrowing serpent. While the warriors hold a defensive circle, Wym and Dexion work to light a roaring bonfire to dispel an unnatural darkness. Upon lighting the fire, a ray of light breaks the storm, and a giant, moth-like [[Principles|Principle]] with feathered wings and long antennae descends from the sun, saying - "Heed my warning, for the fire must be lit for my light to protect you".

Yaku reveals this story is the origin of Heed's annual Festival of Dousing, albeit with a few tweaks to the characters involved in order to make the party feel more welcomed. However, this year's festival is suspended to the influx of refugees.

Chieftain Sthuna eventually meets the party, accompanied by the arrival of an [[Eikavox|eikavox]] - a mysterious, music-playing box gifted by [[Ekklesbraght]]. Sthuna presents the party with their primary mission: the nearby hamlet of [[Scarp]] has been overrun by a flying Principle and its minions. The hamlet is built on a defensive escarpment, but the flying nature of the threat caught the residents unawares, forcing survivors to flee to Heed.

The party spends their final evening in Heed preparing for the journey. Kaelen interviews refugees from Scarp and concludes that while the "bat-like" minions caused casualties, they did not seem entirely bloodthirsty. Owen experiments with [[Alchemical Substances|alchemical ingredients]] in the wilderness. His attempts at [[Sophistry]] result in minor [[Corruption]], manifesting as a persistent hand rash. Heka speaks with Yaku about [[Accord]], home of the [[Consortium of Man]], and learns that the Consortium's literacy program is responsible for Yaku's transition from oral tradition to written records. Marko searches for trade opportunities but finds the refugees have little to offer. Scarlet searches for silk among the tents but finds only common linen.

The following morning, after an uneasy night for those afflicted by nightmares, the party sets off toward Scarp. Despite Bodin's desire for a hasty march, the group decides on a cautious march to avoid being caught by a Principle ambush.

As the party reaches the ridgeline overlooking Scarp, they witness a scene of devastation: many of the hamlet's thatched-roof houses have caved inward, and several large, bat-like creatures circle the village in organized patrol shifts. Despite Bodin's urge for a hasty assault, the group maintains a cautious approach, leaving their donkeys and heavy gear tied up outside the village to improve their stealth. Marko takes the lead, identifying that the creatures possess fur, leathery wings, and hornet-like stingers.

While sneaking through the ruins, the party turns a corner and is confronted by a smaller, flightless version of the creatures. This juvenile appears non-hostile and hobbles toward them in an inquisitive manner. Heka attempts a peaceful gesture by offering it dried meat, but the creature seems unresponsive to the food. During this standoff, Owen's keen sight allows him to spot movement in a nearby ditch.

The tension is shattered when a blast of fire erupts from the ditch, targeting the juvenile creature. Interpreting this as a signal for combat, Marko and Scarlet charge forward. Bodin joins the fray, but it is Owen who delivers the final blow, swiftly felling the creature with a well-aimed arrow.

In the ditch, the party discovers Tanji, a man whose body is horrifically disintegrating, with his flesh literally falling off to reveal bone. He desperately urges the party into a nearby cellar and onto a perfectly flush metal circle inlaid in the stone floor, claiming it is the only place "they" cannot sense him. While Heka uses his healing arts to ease the man's suffering, Kaelen and Owen investigate the cellar, finding notes detailing Tanji's failed attempts to locate other survivors and the symptoms of his magical corruption.

Recognizing Bodin as the strongest warrior, the dying Tanji uses a bony finger to draw a black, oily triangle on the hunter's forehead, granting him a temporary, mystical surge of strength. Immediately after this act, Tanji's condition worsens rapidly; he loses his limbs and his throat before he can offer more warnings. He frantically gestures for the party to flee just as the cellar begins to creak and moan.

The party bolts for the exit as the entire house implodes behind them, collapsing into a heap of rubble. The massive noise alerts the patrolling Principle and its minions, who rally with piercing screeches and begin to descend upon the party.
