The party figures the most likely place to what [[Scarlet]] saw in her vision of [[Amber]] was the village of [[Ferrun]]. After many days' travel, they arrive to a village of sturdy stonework, heavy smoke, and the constant sound of bellows and hammers. Outside the gates, they encounter a group of workers, including a man named Nikolas, loading heavy crates of metal destined for [[Ekklesbraght]]. During this interaction, the party learns that the town's mining has been halted because the rock has begun to "bleed" lava after only a few strikes of a pickaxe - a phenomenon that started three days prior. Crucially, the workers confirm that a hardworking redhead matching Amber's description arrived a week ago and had been working in the mines.

Upon entering Ferrun, the party witnesses a disturbance at a mine entrance where an older man reprimands his son for nearly dying to a sudden lava breach. By identifying the home with the ornate, copper trefoil hanging above its door, the party finds elder master crafter and chieftain of Ferrun, Bolvar. They inform him of the destruction of [[Heed]] and [[Scarp]], and in return, Bolvar explains that the recent mining surge was driven by Ekklesbraght's demands for metal to build their new city. He also mentions that the [[Ekklesbraght Foundation]] provided [[Alchemical Substances|alchemical]] "blast stones" to speed up production, which may have contributed to the tremors and lava issues.

While at Bolvar's home, [[Owen]] presents the [[eikavox]]. Bolvar identifies the exterior metalwork as the craft of an artisan of Ferrun named Talon who now resides in Ekklesbraght, but the internal crystal rod remains a mystery. Owen experiments by striking the rod with a knife, causing the box to emit a blast of harsh noise and physically shudder.

The party secures lodging at the Pig's Trough inn, where the innkeeper, Margaret, recognizes Scarlet due to her resemblance to Amber. Amber has been missing for several days and owes a debt for her room. Upon searching Amber's room, the party discovers a shattered quartz tube, a bronze horse trinket, and a mysterious circular pattern etched into the floor dust. During the search, [[Marko]] picks up a piece of quartz that begins to glow, identifying it as a magical [[Beacon|beacon]]. That night, [[Kaelen]] and [[Owen]] both experience vivid nightmares - Kaelen of the sea and Owen of running through endless rabbit holes.

The following morning, the party meets Misha, the mine supervisor, who is frustrated by the forced work stoppage. He takes them into the tunnels to demonstrate the danger, striking a wall that appears solid but immediately oozes magma that eventually cools and mends the aperture. As they discuss the missing miners, including Amber, they hear rustling behind them. The rock wall begins to crumble and a bright, glowing arm punctures through the stone from the inside.
