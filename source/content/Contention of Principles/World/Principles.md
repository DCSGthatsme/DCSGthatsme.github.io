Principles are powerful, capricious, and supernatural beings of world-shaping influence that roam the wild. Their appearances are diverse and inhuman. Principles possess [[Mystical Powers|mystical abilities]] as an innate characteristic. Unlike humans, they do not incur [[Corruption]] for their magic. 

Most human tribes view Principles with bitter hate or resigned apathy. It is common belief that the [[Shalands|Shafolk]] practice reverence or outright submission. Humanity's strategy for surviving against them often depends on simple avoidance - settling in remote regions or areas where Principles are rarely found.

With a Principle's recognition, a [[Sophistry|Sophist]] can form a [[Principle Bonds|Principle Bond]].
